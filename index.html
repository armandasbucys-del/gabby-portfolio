PROJECT / 002 IB CHEMISTRY

The chemistry of
vitamin C.

↗

IB Chemistry Internal Assessment · Independent Research at Klaipėda Lyceum

Examining how different heating methods (hot plate versus microwave) and temperatures influence vitamin C concentration in orange juice. This controlled study used iodometric titration, standardized iodine solutions, and statistical analysis to examine the relationship between thermal exposure and nutrient stability.

01 / THE BIG PICTURE

Overall research

Our research group investigates the rheological behavior of dense suspensions, focusing on how applied shear stress influences viscosity, particle interactions, and the overall flow behavior of these materials.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

My research focus

My work focuses on how individual particles respond to shear stress — how they rearrange, interact, and form ordered structures. Using simulation data and MATLAB, I analyze particle positions, frictional contacts, and structural changes to understand their relationship with the suspension's overall behavior.
